Most people who give up on Effect do not give up because of the API. They give
up because they keep writing TypeScript the way they always have, with an Effect
wrapper around it. It costs more and gives nothing back.

This track is about that change in thinking. It has four short pages. Each page
takes one habit you already have, shows what the habit hides, and names the
replacement. The track does not tour the library, and no page needs Effect
experience.

Read this track, then continue with [Basic Effect](/learn/basic-effect) to learn
the API and build a complete program.
